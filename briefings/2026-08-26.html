Indonesia's first aircraft carrier will debut at the TNI anniversary, while the carrier base enters its finishing phase.

President Prabowo has set an ambitious target for Indonesia to stop importing oil and LPG within three years. In parallel, the government launched a massive solar power initiative, with Indonesia setting a three-year target for 100 GW solar capacity, a project attracting US$62 billion in investment. The 100 GWp solar plant programme is expected to save the state Rp 73.9 trillion per year through reduced solar fuel imports and generate 5.5 million jobs with Rp 1,140 trillion investment. The government is also working to power off-grid villages with 100 GWp solar plants.
