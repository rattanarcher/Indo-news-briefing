Domestic Politics

The DPR has formed a special working group (Panja) to supervise the investigation of three corruption cases involving Febrie Adriansyah, following the dramatic overnight resignation and naming of the former chief prosecutor for special crimes (Jampidsus) as a suspect. Commission III chairman Habiburokhman stated the oversight body would monitor search operations to ensure evidence integrity amid concerns gold bars could be switched with chocolate, while urging the Attorney General's Office to form an independent investigative team with no affiliation to Febrie. The case has been classified as mega-corruption and will be supervised by the KPK.
