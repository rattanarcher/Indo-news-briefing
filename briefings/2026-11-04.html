Domestic Politics

Indonesia's national police have named former National Police Chief Listyo Sigit and two other senior officers as suspects in an illegal wiretapping operation targeting President Prabowo Subianto. The wiretapping allegedly began in 2025 while Listyo was still police chief, with targets including the president, ministers, and business figures. Listyo, who was appointed Presidential Chief of Staff just a month ago, has been detained at National Police headquarters and faces up to 10 years imprisonment.
