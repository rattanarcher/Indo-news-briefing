Domestic Politics

President Prabowo has ordered the acceleration of Prambanan temple restoration from an estimated 200 years to just three years, with Culture Minister Fadli Zon explaining the project will create jobs for tradespeople and vocational school graduates. The restoration may use new stones if originals are unavailable, Fadli added. Separately, Prabowo attended the 79th National Cooperatives Day peak event and met with Luhut to discuss economic conditions and GovTech.

Indonesia also urged the US and Iran to respect the Islamabad Memorandum amid escalating tensions in the region.
