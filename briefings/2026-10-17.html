The KPK detained Klaten Bupati Hamenang Wajar Ismoyo and Central Java BPK head after naming them suspects in a major corruption case. KPK investigators seized Rp 934 million in cash from a blue barrel at the bupati's brother's home, part of a wider scheme involving routine payments from local government entities totaling Rp 1.8 billion.
