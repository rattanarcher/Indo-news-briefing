What Happened Last Week

President Prabowo proposed Destry Damayanti as sole candidate for Bank Indonesia governor and floated amnesty for SOE executives in his major annual address.

On Monday, August 10, Prabowo nominated acting governor Destry Damayanti as the only candidate to permanently lead Bank Indonesia following Perry Warjiyo's sudden resignation in late July, potentially making her the first woman to hold the post. The rupiah strengthened following the announcement, with markets relieved that concerns about central bank independence had been allayed. Later in the week, during his Friday speech at the annual MPR session, Prabowo floated creating an ad hoc court to investigate SOE executives over the past 30 years while also proposing amnesty for those who "repent" as part of a broader plan to close 750 unproductive state companies. On Saturday, State Secretary Prasetyo Hadi clarified that the proposal aimed at governance reform had not yet reached the amnesty stage, though coalition deputy speaker Dasco Ahmad said the idea needed further study as Prabowo was "too enthusiastic."

Celebrations marking 21 years of peace in Aceh descended into violence when crowds clashed with security forces over a crescent-star flag.

On Saturday, August 15, clashes erupted at Banda Aceh's Baiturrahman Grand Mosque during commemorations of Aceh Peace Day when an individual climbed a flagpole and lowered Indonesia's red-and-white flag to raise a crescent-star flag. TNI personnel on guard fired warning shots into the air when crowds attempted to raise the separatist banner, provoking stone-throwing and a chase of security forces toward the city parliament building. Several vehicles were damaged and at least one motorcycle was burned in the unrest. Army spokesman Major General Muhammad Nas called for all parties to respect state symbols and maintain order, while former vice president Jusuf Kalla urged restraint and emphasized the 21st anniversary as a moment to build justice and prosperity in Aceh.

Indonesia's navy conducted a passing exercise with China near Taiwan, drawing condemnation from Taipei and requests for clarification.

On Tuesday, August 12, China's defense ministry announced that Chinese and Indonesian naval vessels would conduct a joint navigation exercise in waters east of Taiwan, involving communications drills and at-sea replenishment. The exercise took place Wednesday morning between 6:30 a.m. and 9:30 a.m., roughly 70-80 nautical miles east of Taiwan, featuring Indonesia's frigate KRI I Gusti Ngurah Rai and China's Honghe. Taiwan's foreign ministry asked Indonesia for clarification and condemned China's behavior as creating a false impression of jurisdiction over the waters. Indonesian Navy spokesman First Admiral Tunggul explained that the exercise was routine maritime diplomacy conducted while the warship returned from Vladivostok, emphasizing that passing exercises are common goodwill engagements conducted by navies worldwide. Analysts noted this was the first foreign navy involvement since China began patrols off Taiwan's east coast in June.

Expert Commentary This Week

Writing in Fulcrum, Yanuar Nugroho argues that Indonesia's revised financial omnibus law reflects a broader trend towards executive recentralisation, with the central government reconsolidating authority through institutional control. The amendment to the P2SK Law expands Bank Indonesia's mandate while increasing parliamentary oversight, part of a pattern that includes regional budget cuts, sovereign wealth fund governance, and data regulation changes. Nugroho contends that institutions previously entrusted to protect long-term stability are now increasingly expected to deliver short-term political objectives, eroding the deliberate distribution of power established during Reformasi in the late 1990s.

While Indonesia's Navy downplayed the significance of the drill as routine, the incident reflects a recent trend of China leveraging third countries to corroborate its jurisdictional claims, though it remains unclear whether Indonesia fully understood the sensitivity beforehand.
