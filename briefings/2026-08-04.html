The KPK issued six SP3 terminations for corruption cases, including one involving the DPR Secretary General. The move marks a significant development in the anti-corruption body's case management. This comes alongside other investigative actions as the KPK continues to examine various corruption cases across the country.

Foreign Policy & Defence

President Prabowo hosted Thai Prime Minister Anutin in the first official visit by a Thai PM to Indonesia in 15 years. The leaders set a target of US$20 billion in bilateral trade by 2030 and adopted a strategic partnership roadmap to 2030. Both countries also launched new flight routes to boost tourism.

  • The Royal Thai Armed Forces awarded an honorary military decoration to President Prabowo, whom Thai military officials described as a "military icon".
  • Thailand committed to supply 36 amphibious armored vehicles for Indonesia's Marine Corps.
  • Indonesia and Thailand agreed to strengthen cooperation against transnational crime, with PM Anutin telling Prabowo that Thailand had rescued 1,000 Indonesian scam victims.
  • President Prabowo offered condolences on the passing of Thai Princess Bajrakitiyabha.
  • The Foreign Ministry confirmed no Indonesians were affected by a 5.6 magnitude earthquake in Egypt.
