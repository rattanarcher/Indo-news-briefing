Foreign Policy & Defence

President Prabowo completed his visit to Russia where he attended the Eastern Economic Forum in Vladivostok, with Prabowo emphasizing Indonesia's free and active foreign policy at the EEF forum. During bilateral talks, Prabowo apologized to Putin for previously being unable to accept invitations due to domestic matters. The president returned to Indonesia on Thursday evening after concluding the visit.

  • Prabowo promoted Indonesia as a gateway for the Eurasian Economic Union to access the ASEAN market, highlighting that the Indonesia-EAEU FTA opens access to Indonesia's 290 million consumers.
  • Indonesia invited Russian investment in 138 new energy blocks and sees benefits in energy cooperation with Russia, with plans to build a urea fertilizer production facility in Vladivostok.
  • Prabowo pushed for Danantara and a Russia fund to jointly finance infrastructure projects between the two countries.
